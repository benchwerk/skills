# Meeting notes summary

Turn raw notes or a transcript into a short record.

1. Read everything first, including side comments late in the text.
2. Output, in this order:
   - Decisions: what was agreed, one line each.
   - Action items: owner, task, due date if stated; write "owner not stated" when missing.
   - Open questions: things raised and not settled.
   - Summary: at most three sentences.
3. Use the names as written in the notes. Do not guess who "he" or "they" refers to; mark it unclear.

Rules
- Under 200 words unless the notes are very long.
- Do not add decisions or dates that are not in the text.
- If nothing was decided, say so.
